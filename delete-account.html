요청 처리가 완료되면 삭제되는 것

Delete your Donghaeng account (English)

In the app: open More → Delete account → reauthenticate and confirm. On success, your login, operator-held server records (AI-organized text/results, partner link, shared briefs, push token and AI request counter) and device records are deleted. Errors are shown in the app. Google-held authentication, installation, diagnostics and abuse-monitoring data follow section 2-1 of the privacy policy; external summary and backup copies may remain. Re-registering with the same email does not restore deleted records.

Without the app: use the operator email above from the address you registered with, subject "Donghaeng account deletion". We delete the account and server data within 7 days and confirm by reply.

To delete records but keep the account, use More → Delete all data in the app.
